🎯 About KI CTF

Welcome to KI CTF, an exciting Capture The Flag competition designed to challenge your cybersecurity skills and knowledge. This event brings together security enthusiasts, students, and professionals from around the world.

Whether you're a beginner looking to learn or an experienced security professional seeking new challenges, KI CTF offers a dynamic environment to compete, collaborate, and showcase your talents. Join teams, solve challenges, and climb the leaderboard to prove your expertise.
